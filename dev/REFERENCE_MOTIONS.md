# Reference-led motion revision

Reference material:
- CrossFit, The Dumbbell Farmers Carry: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5MNNosenJc
- CrossFit Strongman manual: https://assets.crossfit.com/pdfs/seminars/SMERefs/Strongman/CrossFitStrongmanManual.pdf
- Cornell goblet squat demo index: https://cornellbigred.com/sports/2010/7/8/StrengthConditioningVideoDemonstrations
- NASM goblet squat coaching: https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/training/how-to-perform-goblet-squats
- NASM dumbbell RDL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aa57T45iFSE

The public descriptions/coaching material and linked demonstration sources were verified. This revision is authored 3D animation, not motion capture or video-frame tracking; the sources do not establish exact joint trajectories for this avatar.

Changes:
- Farmer carry: 1.2 m path, small steps, two stepped turns and return. Loop 10.8 s. Stance contacts are anchored in world space; swing feet clear the floor. Arms hang with neutral grips and shoulders remain quiet.
- Goblet squat: planted feet and slight toe-out, more upright trunk, hip and knee motion solved together, vertical dumbbell at chest, closer hand target and downward elbows. Timing follows the existing coaching phase metadata.
- RDL: pelvis and trunk hinge together, limited knee flexion, planted feet, near-straight hanging arms and weights near the legs. Lowering and return follow the phase metadata.

Review: eight sampled phases × three fixed views for each exercise. Regression checks cover stance slip, path travel, loop continuity, planted feet and knee range, plus the existing gear, hand and fault-mode tests. These checks do not establish clinical correctness or physical-device rendering quality.
